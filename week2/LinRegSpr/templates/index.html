Physics Theory

The classical mechanics that this neural network learns from scratch

🌀

Hooke's Law

The restoring force of a spring is directly proportional to its displacement from the equilibrium position. Valid within the elastic limit.

F = −kx
⚖️

Static Equilibrium

When a mass hangs at rest, the spring force exactly balances gravitational force. Net force is zero, giving us the elongation formula.

kx = mg
📐

Elongation Prediction

By combining Hooke's Law and the equilibrium condition we can directly predict the spring extension for any suspended mass.

x = mg / k
